DSAF Lite Benchmark

Privacy policy

Status: applies to the repo-shipped benchmark sandbox and the future production form.

What data we collect

The benchmark asks for 25 DSAF-25 criterion scores, four anonymous demographic buckets, and one optional free-text field.

We do not require name, email, company, website, phone number, precise location, or account creation.

Form vendor

The recommended production vendor is Tally, Typeform, or Formspree with a data-processing agreement enabled. Tally is the preferred configuration because it supports GDPR-oriented form operations and embeddable static pages.

The repo sandbox uses browser session storage only and sends no network request.

How we use the data

Structured responses are aggregated into peer-comparison statistics and may feed the future State of Design System Audits report. Individual responses are not displayed.

Optional free text is reviewed only for qualitative themes and is not published as identifying copy.

Anonymisation and low-N guard

Public results display aggregate statistics only. Segment-level results stay hidden until there are at least 30 responses in the segment.

Data retention

Raw form responses are retained for 12 months, then deleted or re-aggregated into anonymous statistics. Aggregate statistics may remain in public reports.

Your rights under GDPR

You may request access, erasure, rectification, portability, objection, or restriction. Email info@cyberskill.world with your production submission ID. If you did not save the ID and provided no identifying field, we may be unable to locate a specific anonymous response.

Legal basis

Structured anonymous responses are processed under legitimate interest in research and community benchmarking. Optional free-text disclosures are processed by consent.

No paid-funnel use

The benchmark does not require contact details and is not used to sell individual audit services. DSAF commercial services live separately from this benchmark surface.
