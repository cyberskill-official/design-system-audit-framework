## Maker's first comment (post within 5 minutes of go-live)

```
Hi PH 👋

I'm Stephen, founder of CyberSkill — a software consultancy in Ho Chi Minh City. I built DSAF because every design-system "is it any good" conversation I had was a taste argument with no shared rubric.

DSAF is the rubric. It's MIT licensed, with a 25-row Core that fits on one page (https://audit.cyberskill.world/card) and a full 125-criterion rubric behind it for signed audits.

Three things I want makers/founders here to know:

1. There's no SaaS. The framework IS the markdown files. You score it yourself, an LLM agent can help (the prompts and check scripts are in the repo), and the audit report is a single .md file that diffs across audits.

2. Self-audits cap publicly at L3 until third-party verification. I do this on my own consultancy's worked example. The cap is in the rubric, not in the marketing copy.

3. Critique is the point. Show HN had 100+ comments by hour 8 — the criteria boundaries, the dedup methodology, and the no-silent-regression rule got the most useful pushback. Bring more.

Two asks:

- If you maintain a design system: try the 25-row Core. Tell me what's missing.
- If you've built or audited DS-adjacent tools: the GitHub issues tab is the right place for substantive critique, but reply here too — I'm reading every comment.

No paid CTA, no email-capture form, no upsell. The framework's surface is the framework's surface.

— Stephen
```
