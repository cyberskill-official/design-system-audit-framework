**Repo-verifiable state:** expansion plan ready; translation PRs require community contributors and native review.

2. French and Portuguese: second wave.
3. Full 125-criterion translations only after DSAF-25 terminology stabilizes.

## Scope Expansion

- DSAF-25 Core.

## Review Gate

Every published translation needs:

- Native-speaker reviewer.
- Maintainer approval.
- Terminology consistency pass.
- Hreflang and canonical URL discipline.
