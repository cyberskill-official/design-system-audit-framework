## Working Title

Design-system maturity is not a score. It is an operating model.

## Pitch

Hi Nathan,

DSAF has a public rubric now, but I do not want the next article to be "here is our framework." The better piece is a joint argument about what maturity models get wrong: they over-index on level labels and under-index on the operating routines that make systems safer quarter after quarter.

Would you consider co-authoring a practical piece with me? My contribution would be the DSAF evidence model and agent-native audit loop. Yours, if you are interested, could be the hard-won governance and decision-shaping perspective from years of design-system work.

No launch-praise ask, no endorsement requirement. If the piece makes DSAF sharper by disagreeing with it in public, that is a win.

## Outline

1. Why maturity labels are attractive and dangerous.
2. What evidence a serious design-system audit needs.
3. Where criteria help and where they flatten judgment.
4. How recurring rituals beat annual theater.
5. A lightweight operating model teams can copy.
