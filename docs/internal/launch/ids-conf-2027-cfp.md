# TASK-LAUNCH-007 - Into Design Systems Conf 2027 CFP

**Talk title:** Agent-native design system audits: what 50 audits taught us about MCP-readiness **Format:** 30-minute conference talk or 45-minute workshop. **Repo-verifiable state:** submission packet ready; CFP portal submission is external.

## Abstract

Design-system audits usually collapse into expert taste: useful, expensive, and hard to rerun. DSAF turns audit work into a criteria-based system: evidence, citations, human review, no-silent-regression records, and repeatable DSAF Levels.

This talk shares the patterns we expect from 50 audits: where mature systems hide risk, how agent workflows change evidence gathering, why MCP-readiness belongs inside design-system governance, and where humans still must sign the result.

Attendees leave with a 25-row audit card they can run in five minutes and a map for turning findings into a fix cycle instead of a one-off deck.

## Outline

1. Why design-system audits become taste arguments.
2. The DSAF-25 Core: a five-minute first pass.
3. What "agent-native" means without turning the audit into oracle theater.
4. MCP-readiness: file surfaces, rules, evidence, permissions, and review loops.
5. Three examples: tokens, documentation freshness, and no-silent-regression.
6. What still needs humans: consent, governance, certification, and signed scores.
