# TASK-LAUNCH-008 - zeroheight 2026 Design Systems Report Pitch

**Target section:** tooling, measurement, or governance. **Repo-verifiable state:** pitch ready; inclusion decision external.

## Pitch

Hi zeroheight team,

Your recent reports have named the measurement gap clearly: design-system tooling is improving, but measurement remains fractured. DSAF is an open-source attempt to give that gap a shared rubric.

DSAF is not a zeroheight competitor in the platform sense. It is a criteria layer: 25-row Core for first-pass scoring, 125 criteria for signed audits, no-silent-regression tracking, and integrations that read local Storybook, Tokens Studio, and zeroheight export evidence.

If the 2026 report includes a tooling or measurement section, I would value consideration for a short mention or sidebar:

- What DSAF measures: system maturity and produced-UX maturity.
- How it differs from SaaS platforms: repo-native, open, citation-based.
- Why it matters: gives DS teams a repeatable audit artifact across quarters.

Happy to share anonymized benchmark patterns once `benchmark.audit.cyberskill.world` has enough low-N-safe responses.
