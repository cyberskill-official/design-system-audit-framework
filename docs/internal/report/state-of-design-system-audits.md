# TASK-REPORT-001 - Annual State of Design System Audits Report

**Repo-verifiable state:** report structure ready; public charts require benchmark data and privacy review.

## Report Structure

1. Executive summary.
2. Methodology and low-N privacy guard.
3. DSAF-25 median and quartiles.
4. Strongest categories.
5. Weakest categories.
6. Adoption and governance patterns.
7. Accessibility and performance gaps.
8. Agent-readiness and MCP-readiness.
9. What changed since last year.
10. Public dataset notes.

## Data Sources

- Lite benchmark aggregate data.
- Hosted benchmark aggregate data.
- Public DSAF audit reports.
- Voluntary maintainer survey.

## Privacy Guard

No chart ships if a segment has fewer than 30 responses. Free-text quotes require explicit written consent and may be paraphrased instead of quoted.
