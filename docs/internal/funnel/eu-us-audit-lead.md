# TASK-FUNNEL-004 - EU/US Audit Lead Partner Playbook

**Repo-verifiable state:** partner profile, screening script, and operating model ready; hiring/partnering is external.

## Partner Profile

- Based in EU, UK, or US.
- Credible design-system audit, governance, accessibility, or platform-design background.
- Comfortable fronting client calls while CyberSkill delivers analysis and implementation support.
- No conflict that prevents vendor-neutral DSAF recommendations.

## Screening Questions

1. Which design-system maturity model do you trust least, and why?
2. How would you explain DSAF's self-audit cap to a skeptical VP?
3. What evidence would make you refuse to sign an audit?
4. Are you comfortable saying "we cannot certify this yet" to a paying buyer?

## Operating Model

- Audit lead owns buyer trust, kickoff, executive readout, and final sign-off.
- CyberSkill owns evidence processing, analysis support, and remediation delivery where separately scoped.
- Revenue share is negotiated per engagement; no hidden reseller markup in the methodology docs.
