# TASK-FUNNEL-003 - Pricing and Lead Magnet

**Repo-verifiable state:** pricing policy and self-score CSV ready.

## Price Floor

| Offer | Price |
|---|---:|
| Tier 1 DSAF-25 third-party audit | USD 4,900 floor |
| Full DSAF-125 audit | Scoped quote after evidence review |
| Retainer for quarterly re-audit | USD 1,500/month floor |

Do not sell a USD 2,500 Tier 1 audit after this policy. Underpricing signals freelance implementation rather than certified methodology work.

## Lead Magnet

The free lead magnet is a DSAF-25 self-scoring spreadsheet or CSV. It must repeat the self-audit cap: public self-scores cap at L3 without third-party verification.

CSV columns:

`criterion_id,criterion,score_0_to_5,evidence_url,confidence,notes`

## Sales Boundary

The free self-score produces education and fit, not a hidden sales form. If the respondent wants a signed score, route them to `/audits/`.
