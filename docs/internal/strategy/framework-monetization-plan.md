# Framework Monetization Plan

**Status:** strategic source document, not generated audit output.  
**Boundary:** generated `ANALYZED_DESIGN_REPORT.md` and `IMPROVED_DESIGN.md` files must not include commercial packaging, pricing, lead magnets, or service upsell recommendations. Keep commercial strategy here or in other explicit strategy documents.

---

## 1. Principles

- Keep DSAF open, useful, and credible without requiring a paid service.
- Monetize implementation help, evidence operations, enterprise integration, and recurring assurance rather than access to the rubric itself.
- Never let commercial positioning weaken scoring honesty.
- Separate self-serve open-source artifacts from paid service collateral.
- Avoid invented endorsements, unverified results, or inflated maturity claims.

---

## 3. Productized Service Packages

### Baseline Audit

- Inputs: doctrine, docs, tokens, package metadata, Storybook or equivalent, prior audits, release notes.
- Output: `ANALYZED_DESIGN_REPORT.md`, signed executive summary, prioritized roadmap, evidence caveats.
- Human proof: limited calibration, not a full third-party certification.

### Maximal Enterprise Audit

- Inputs: direct file or URL plus repo artifacts when available.
- Output: 371-row enterprise criterion report, AUTO/MANUAL split, artifact recreation plan, standards/source watch, procurement caveats.
- Human proof: manual AT/counsel/independent audit backlog clearly separated.

### Implementation Sprint

- Inputs: approved findings.
- Output: doctrine patches, package/doc/script changes, regenerated artifacts, verification log, migration notes.
- Guardrail: no fix without written approval and rollback path.

### Evidence Operations Retainer

- Inputs: prior report, live repo, release cadence, benchmark watch.
- Output: monthly/quarterly evidence refresh, stale-claim cleanup, generated output regeneration, executive delta report.
- Guardrail: no public maturity claim upgrade without evidence.

---

## 4. Differentiators To Package

- Unified AUTO/MANUAL enterprise criterion table.
- Direct `DESIGN.md` and public URL scanning modes.
- Source-preserving `IMPROVED_DESIGN.md`.
- Strict generated-output contract.
- Evidence appendix with dated citation refs.
- Artifact recreation and no-silent-regression gates.
- Large-enterprise coverage beyond tokens/components: legal, AI, accessibility, sustainability, procurement, security, support, localization, and design operations.

---

## 5. Sales And Trust Collateral

- Public README remains neutral and open-source first.
- Case studies must state scope, evidence, date, and limitations.
- Reviewer quotes require written consent before publication.
- Commercial pages should link to the public methodology and clarify what is free.
- Procurement one-pager should avoid maturity claims unless backed by a signed report.

---

## 7. Non-Goals

- Do not gate the public rubric behind a service.
- Do not put upsell content into generated audit outputs.
- Do not imply legal, accessibility, or security certification from heuristic scans.
- Do not claim third-party validation before it exists.
- Do not mix client-private evidence into public examples without approval.

*End of strategy document.*
