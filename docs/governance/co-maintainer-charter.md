# DSAF co-maintainer charter

**Status:** role charter.
**FR:** FR-GOV-002.

## Role overview

The co-maintainer role is OSS-volunteer-equivalent, not formal employment.
It does not include salary, equity, a board seat, fiduciary duty, or employer/employee status.

## Rights

1. GitHub Maintain access after written acceptance.
2. Governance vote in future RFC cycles.
3. audit.cyberskill.world blog authorship.
4. Speaking-slot revenue share where a DSAF talk receives honoraria.
5. Public recognition as DSAF maintainer.
6. Co-author credit on collaborative content.

## Responsibilities

1. Review RFCs within 14 days when assigned.
2. Review external-contributor PRs in area of expertise.
3. Share selected weekly deep-dive authorship when expertise matches.
4. Represent DSAF publicly when mutually agreed.
5. Honor DSAF handle taxonomy, self-audit cap, and decoupling policy.
6. Disclose availability constraints and conflicts.

## Commitment and exit

The expected commitment is 12 months.
Either side may exit with 30-day notice and a transition plan.

## Conflict of interest

- Own DSAF-related consulting work must be disclosed.
- Cross-company collaborations that may bias a criterion decision must be disclosed.
- Speaking-slot conflicts or sponsor restrictions must be disclosed before acceptance.

## Decision-making

Candidates are not publicly attributed before written acceptance.
Announcement happens only after the candidate accepts in writing and co-signs the announcement post.

## Amendment

Material changes require maintainer agreement and a public governance note.

*End of co-maintainer charter.*
