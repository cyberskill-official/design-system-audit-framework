## Pitch template

```text
Subject: Pitch: Design-system audits need criteria, not taste

Hi <editor>,

I would like to pitch a practical piece on design-system audits.
The thesis: as design systems absorb accessibility, performance, AI-agent rules, and governance risk, a taste-based maturity review is no longer enough.

The piece would use DSAF, an MIT-licensed open rubric, as the working example without turning the article into product marketing.
It would include the DSAF-25 Core card, three concrete audit examples, and the no-silent-regression policy.

Proposed length: 1,800-2,400 words.
No paid CTA.

Stephen Cheng
```
