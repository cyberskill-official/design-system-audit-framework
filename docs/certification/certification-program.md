# FR-CERT-001 - DSAF Certification

**Repo-verifiable state:** certification tiers and badge rules ready; issuance requires live governance and reviewer capacity.

## Tiers

| Tier | Cost | Meaning |
|---|---:|---|
| Self-attested | Free | Team completed DSAF-25 or DSAF-125 internally. Public cap: L3. |
| Third-party verified | Paid | Approved auditor verified evidence and signed the report. Public cap: L4. |
| Verified plus L5 gate | Paid | Third-party verification plus L5 entry-gate stack. Public cap: L5. |

## Badge Rules

- Badge must include method, date, version, scope, and cap.
- Badge must link to a public or private signed report.
- Self-attested badges cannot imply CyberSkill or DSAF maintainer approval.

## Anti-Abuse

If a team misuses a badge, maintainers can revoke listing from any public registry. The team may still self-publish its own claim, but DSAF will not amplify it.
