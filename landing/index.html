DSAF Levels and the publication cap

§ 03

The L0–L5 ladder maps a combined score to a maturity level. Published self-audits cap at L3 until third-party verification — separately, interior scores stay honest across the full 0–5 scale.

Self-audit publication cap

DSAF's credibility rests on the rubric being independently re-runnable. An author or consultancy self-scoring at the top tier is unverifiable by definition. The cap prevents an unverified top-tier claim from becoming the first thing readers remember.

VerificationMax cited level
No third-party verificationL3 — Managed
Verified by a DSAF-certified verifierL4 — Advanced, verified
Verified + L5 entry-gate stackL5 — Optimised, verified

Combined percentages may appear inside audit data tables where they are calibration data — never as the lead public claim.
